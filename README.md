# Koma

A free, local, AI-powered manga and illustration studio for Ubuntu. Free and open source (MIT).

> **Heads up:** work in progress. Stuff may break or change. Use at your own risk.

Koma is also a concept project: an idea of what a free, local, AI-powered studio could be.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/home.png" width="900" alt="Koma home: manga canvas and illustration canvas"></p>

## Quick start

```bash
git clone https://github.com/the-lord-liam/koma.git
cd koma && bin/koma
```

- First launch adds **Koma** to your app grid.
- Paste your xAI key in **Settings** ([console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai)) for drawing and story AI.
- No key? Outline, lettering, and export still work.

## What's inside

- **Cast:** draw a portrait once, faces stay on-model after.
- **Places:** draw or import backgrounds, attach to panels.
- **3D:** build or import GLB/VRM/OBJ, pose it, add expressions, spin a turntable.
- **Pages:** pick a layout, draw panel by panel, each one matches the last.
- **Read:** flip spreads, right-to-left or left-to-right.
- **Export:** PNG, chapter PDF, CBZ.
- **Illustration:** one picture, no panels.
- **Grok bot:** a chat window with your own bot crew.

## Tools and keys

- **1 Lasso:** circle a spot, redraw just that.
- **2 Select:** move and resize balloons and things.
- **3 Text:** AI speech bubbles, still editable.
- **4 Sketch:** doodle, AI cleans it up.
- **5 Effects:** screentone, speed lines, rain, night...
- **6 Imagine:** prompt a panel or a whole page.
- **7 Lines:** pull clean line art, runs locally.
- **8 Draw:** live AI art as you sketch. **Tab** = full split screen.
- **9 Tone / 0 Color:** screentone or color a circled area.
- **Storyboard:** see the whole chapter as a grid.
- **Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z** undo/redo, **[ ]** move panels, **M** mirror pose, **R** references, **?** all shortcuts.

## Privacy

- No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking. Ever.
- Your books, art, and key stay on your computer.
- Network only when you use AI features (xAI, or optional Meshy/Tripo if you pick them).
